# Hi, I'm Aysel 👋

Electrical engineering student at **Florida Atlantic University** (dual enrollment) and founder of **ThermoVolt Marine**. I like problems that go unnoticed — wasted heat on ships, hearts that need monitoring, oceans that need protecting.

## ⚡ What I'm building

**[ThermoVolt Marine (TVM)](https://github.com/aysel-elsh/thermovolt-marine)** — solid-state thermoelectric system that turns marine engine waste heat into electricity and cools discharge water.
- Bench results: 512 W heat removed, 7.33 °C discharge temperature drop, 7.81 V output (15 trials, p < 0.001)
- Patent pending · Zenodo preprint · abstract accepted to IOCMSE 2026 (MDPI)
- FAU Runway Accelerator · FAU WAVE funding · 2026 National Green Difference Award

**Medical device hardware @ Aventusoft** — PCB design from schematic to soldering and test, wireless charger design, hardware lead on a miniature cardiac-monitoring wearable.

**Research @ FAU** — underwater robotics + XR teleoperation (Meta Quest 3 × BlueROV2), and thermodynamic / probabilistic computing experiments on Raspberry Pi.

## 🔓 Open source

**[tegkit](https://github.com/aysel-elsh/tegkit)**: a dependency-free Python toolkit for modelling thermoelectric generators (power, efficiency, ZT, I-V curves, Peltier cooling, heat-sink sizing, real-module presets, CLI). Contributors welcome; check the *good first issue* label.

**[pbit-energy-bench](https://github.com/aysel-elsh/pbit-energy-bench)**: does probabilistic-bit (p-bit) sampling actually save energy on a Raspberry Pi? An INA219-measured benchmark against a small neural net (in progress).

**[portfolio](https://github.com/aysel-elsh/portfolio)**: source for my personal site, live at [aysel-elshenawy.vercel.app](https://aysel-elshenawy.vercel.app) (Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind).

**[cs50-final-project](https://github.com/aysel-elsh/cs50-final-project)**: my Harvard CS50x final project (2022), a command-line calculator in C.
